What kind of systems do you build?

Records and management systems, POS and inventory, appointment and document request portals, and responsive websites.

Do you work with small stores and barangays?

Yes. SariSari OS and BrgyDocs PH were built with a pilot sari-sari store and a pilot barangay in mind.

SariSari OS and eSKUL are designed offline-first, syncing later when a connection is available.
